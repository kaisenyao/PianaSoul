Let this phone trust your Mac让手机信任这台 Mac

Once per phone. PianaSoul's page is private to your home network; this certificate lets the phone open it safely.每部手机只需一次。PianaSoul 的页面只在你家的网络里；装上这个证书，手机才能安全地打开它。

Download the certificate下载证书

Certificate: “{{name}}”证书名称：“{{name}}”

iPhone

  1. Tap Download the certificate, then Allow.
  2. 点下载证书，再点允许。
  3. Open Settings: tap Profile Downloaded near the top, then Install.
  4. 打开设置，点顶部的已下载描述文件，再点安装。
  5. Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings: turn on full trust for “{{name}}”.
  6. 设置 › 通用 › 关于本机 › 证书信任设置：打开“{{name}}”的完全信任。

Easier still: send the certificate from the Mac with AirDrop (PianaSoul's settings, Phone).更省事的办法：在 Mac 上用隔空投送发过来（PianaSoul 设置 › 手机）。

Android

  1. Tap Download the certificate.
  2. 点下载证书。
  3. Open Settings › Security › Encryption & credentials › Install a certificate › CA certificate, and choose the file you downloaded. (The way there differs a little from one phone to another: search Settings for “certificate”.)
  4. 打开设置 › 安全 › 加密与凭据 › 安装证书 › CA 证书，选刚下载的文件。（各品牌的路径略有不同，可以在设置里搜索“证书”。）

Then然后

Scan the second code on the Mac to open PianaSoul, and add it to the Home Screen.扫 Mac 上的第二个二维码打开 PianaSoul，再添加到主屏幕。

If your router gives the Mac a new address, the Home Screen app has to be added again. Reserving the Mac's address in the router avoids that.如果路由器给 Mac 换了地址，主屏幕上的 App 就要重新添加。在路由器里给 Mac 保留固定地址可以避免这种情况。
