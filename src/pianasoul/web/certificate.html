Let This Phone Trust Your Mac让手机信任这台Mac

Once per phone. PianaSoul's page is private to your home network; this certificate lets the phone open it safely.每部手机只需设置一次。PianaSoul的页面仅在你的家庭网络中可用；安装此证书后，手机才能安全地打开它。

  2. 轻点下载证书，然后轻点允许。
  3. Open Settings, tap Profile Downloaded near the top, then Install. Not there? Look in General › VPN & Device Management, or download it again.
  4. 打开设置，轻点顶部的已下载描述文件，然后轻点安装。如果没有看到，请前往通用 › VPN与设备管理查找，或重新下载。
  5. Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings: turn on full trust for “{{name}}”. Installing alone is not enough: this step is needed too.
  6. 设置 › 通用 › 关于本机 › 证书信任设置：为“{{name}}”打开完全信任。仅安装证书还不够，必须完成此步骤。

Troubleshooting疑难解答

To be sure the certificate is your Mac's, compare its SHA-256 fingerprint in the phone's certificate details with the one behind the ⓘ in PianaSoul's settings on the Mac (Phone, step 1), or the one the terminal printed when PianaSoul first started there.若要确认证书来自你的Mac，请将手机上证书详细信息中的SHA-256指纹，与Mac上PianaSoul设置（“手机”，第1步）中ⓘ显示的指纹比对；如果在“终端”中运行，则与首次启动时“终端”中显示的指纹比对。

If your router gives the Mac a new address, the Home Screen app has to be added again: reserving the Mac's address in the router avoids that.如果路由器更改了Mac的地址，需要重新添加主屏幕上的App；在路由器中为Mac保留固定地址可避免此问题。
